# Reform -- Product Requirements Document

## 1. About Reform

Reform is a platform where people can buy, sell, give away and reuse
items. It also shares simple recycling tips and gives users a place to
talk about sustainability.

## 2. The Problem

People often throw away things they no longer need, even when those
things could still be useful to someone else. At the same time, some
people need these items but cannot afford to buy them new. Finding
places to recycle certain items can also be difficult.

## 3. The Solution

Reform brings these activities together in one place. Users can sell
things they no longer need, find affordable second-hand items, give
items away for free and learn how to recycle.

## 4. Main Features

### Marketplace

-   View items people have listed for sale.
-   Search for items and browse categories.
-   Check item details, prices and condition.
-   Post, edit and remove personal listings.
-   Save items for later.

### Free Finds

-   View items that people are giving away.
-   Search for free items.
-   Check item details and collection information.
-   Post items to give away.

### Recycling

-   Read simple recycling guides.
-   Learn how to reuse everyday items.
-   Get tips on reducing waste and disposing of items properly.

### Community

-   Share posts and ideas.
-   Read sustainability tips.
-   Share ways to reuse and repair items.

### Profile and Themes

-   Manage personal details and listings.
-   Save favourite items.
-   Change the app's theme and accent colour.

## 5. Who Will Use It?

Reform is for students, everyday users, small sellers and people who
want to save money or reduce waste.

## 6. Design

The app should be simple to use and work on phones, tablets and
computers. It will use pink as its default colour, with other theme
options. The design should be clean, with soft glow effects and readable
text.

## 7. Main Requirements

Users should be able to: - Move between the main sections. - Browse and
search for items. - View item details. - Create sale and free-item
listings. - Read recycling information. - Use the community section. -
Save favourite items. - Change the theme. - Manage their profile.

## 8. First Version

The first version will focus on the main screens and basic experience: -
Home - Marketplace - Free Finds - Recycling - Community - Theme and
colour settings

Features such as full account management, a database, real transactions
and messaging can be added later.

## 9. How Reform Can Make Money

Reform may earn money through promoted listings, small transaction fees,
optional seller plans and partnerships. The Free Finds section should
remain free.

## 10. How Success Will Be Measured

We can look at: - How many people use Reform. - How many items are
listed. - How many items are sold or given away. - How many free items
are collected. - How active the community is. - Whether users continue
using the platform.

## 11. Future Plans

Later versions can add user accounts, saved listings, real buying and
selling, payments, messaging, recycling locations and more community
features.

## 12. Things to Watch Out For

Some users may not trust second-hand items, and some listings may not be
accurate. Clear item descriptions, reporting options and basic rules can
help. Free items should also have clear collection details.
